This policy explains what information ARDOT Consulting ("we") collects through
www.ardotconsulting.com, what we do with it, and the choices you have. We keep
it short because we collect very little.

## What we collect

**When you use the contact form**, we receive what you type: your name, email
address, company (optional), industry (optional) and message. We use it only to
reply to you and, if you ask, to discuss working together.

**When you browse the site**, we count visits with
[Plausible Analytics](https://plausible.io/data-policy). Plausible uses no
cookies, doesn't track you across sites, and doesn't store your IP address; we
only see totals such as page views, referring sites, and the country and device
type of visitors. It also counts a contact-form submission as an event, without
any of the form's contents.

We don't use cookies, advertising trackers or social media pixels, and we don't
store anything in your browser.

## Who handles your information

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't add you to a mailing list.
A few services handle it on our behalf:

- **[FormSubmit.co](https://formsubmit.co/)** passes your contact-form message
  to us by email.
- **[Tuta](https://tuta.com/privacy)** (Germany) hosts the encrypted mailbox
  where your message arrives.
- **[GitHub Pages](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)**
  hosts this website. Like most web hosts, GitHub logs technical details of each
  request, including IP addresses, for security.
- **Plausible Analytics** (EU), as described above.

FormSubmit and GitHub are based in the United States, so your form message may
be processed there.

## Why we use it

We process contact-form information because you asked us to get in touch
(steps before a possible agreement), and site statistics because we have a
legitimate interest in understanding how the site is used without identifying
anyone.

## How long we keep it

We keep contact-form emails for as long as we're in touch with you, and delete
them within 12 months after our last exchange, unless we start working together
and need them for that work. Plausible keeps only aggregate statistics.

## Your rights

You can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and
you can object to how we use it. Email
[ardotconsulting@tutamail.com](mailto:ardotconsulting@tutamail.com) and we'll
reply within 30 days. If you're in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your
data protection authority. If you're in California, note that we don't sell or
share personal information.

This site isn't intended for children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect
their information.

## Proof-of-concept demos

Our blog links to live demos at `*.poc.ardotconsulting.com`. They're public
examples: don't enter real personal or business information into them, as their
data can be reset or removed at any time.
