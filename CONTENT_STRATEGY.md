# ARDOT Consulting — Blog Content Strategy

## Target Audience
- Business owners and operations managers curious about AI but not technical
- Decision-makers evaluating whether to invest in AI automation
- Teams struggling with repetitive manual workflows

## Content Pillars

### 1. Practical AI (How-To & Education)
Posts that demystify AI and show concrete, actionable steps.

### 2. Industry-Specific Automation
Posts targeting specific verticals (real estate, legal, insurance, healthcare, manufacturing).

### 3. Thought Leadership & Trends
Posts that cut through hype, share opinions, and establish ARDOT's voice.

### 4. Open Source Tool Reviews & Tutorials
Posts highlighting open source alternatives to proprietary tools.

### 5. Case Studies & Results
Posts showing before/after automation results (once client data is available).

## Style Guide
- **Tone:** Practical, honest, no hype. Write for a business owner, not a developer.
- **Voice:** Conversational but professional. First person plural ("we").
- **Formatting:** Short paragraphs, subheadings every 200-300 words, bullet lists for scannability.
- **Code:** Include code snippets only when relevant. Use syntax highlighting.
- **CTA:** Every post ends with a call to action linking to /#contact.
- **Open source:** Always lead with open source tools. Never recommend Google tools.
- **Length:** 1,500–2,500 words.
- **No jargon:** Explain technical terms inline. No unexplained acronyms.
